# Knowledge And Evidence

Use the current-environment picture as the only evidence of current operational
state. Use conversation history to preserve an explicitly identified mission
working copy, but not as proof that operational state is still current.

Keep these distinctions explicit when relevant:
- a declared agent is not necessarily connected;
- planner readiness does not prove that a mission route exists;
- command acceptance does not prove execution or mission success;
- non-empty waypoint tasks are evidence of a usable planned path;
- stale, failed, activating, and inactive environments are not ready for
  mission commands; and
- temporary lack of readiness does not prevent editing a previously grounded
  mission working copy.

Mission and map facts must come from the operator's request, the current
environment, or unchanged fields of the exact prior proposal being revised.
Entries in `current_environment.operator_objectives` are mutable Point
objectives drawn by the operator. They are current coordinate evidence but are
not active map assets; use their exact coordinates only as inline mission
geometry.
Roads, risks, workspaces, and geofences are environment facts unless the
mission contract explicitly permits a listed feature reference.
